---
description: Pet your shellmate buddy
---

Run the pet command to pet your buddy and see an affectionate response.

```bash
python3 -m shellmate --pet
```

Your buddy will respond based on its species:
- **cat**: purrs softly
- **dog**: wags tail happily
- **owl**: hoots softly
- **blob**: wobbles gently

Petting your buddy will make it happy for 10 seconds and increment the pet counter. You can see how many times your buddy has been petted with `--whoami`.
